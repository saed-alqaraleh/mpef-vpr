\paragraph{Reproducibility and integrity controls.}
The MPEF experimental protocol fixes the 21-feature evidence contract, candidate
depths, model-selection split and independent calibration split. The two
validation-selected backend models and held-out calibration maps are identified
by SHA-256 digests. Candidate ranking and calibrated probability assessment
are evaluated separately. The archived Tokyo 24/7 retrieval-only experiment
uses the corrected 147-query, 16,140-gallery protocol and official junk handling.

\paragraph{Supplementary materials.}
The internal research archive contains fitted MPEF parameter records, calibration
maps, a 21-feature implementation, automated software checks and per-query
Tokyo 24/7 retrieval-only metric tables. It does not redistribute the source
images, third-party pretrained matching checkpoints or complete raw protected
candidate-level matching evidence.

\paragraph{Data availability.}
Benchmark images and upstream neural-network checkpoints are available only
from their original distributors under their respective terms; this internal
archive provides derived, limited-scope result records.

\paragraph{Code availability.}
An internal, privately hosted MPEF implementation has been prepared and
software-verified. Public release is subject to rights-holder authorization
and final scientific audit. Do not cite the private GitHub URL as a publicly
available reproducibility resource before public release is authorized.
